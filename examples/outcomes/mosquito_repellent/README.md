## Brief

The research brief is at [`../../briefs/mosquito_repellent_brief.json`](../../briefs/mosquito_repellent_brief.json).

**Research objectives:**
1. Understand how mosquitoes disrupt sleep and what consumers do about it
2. Map the product category landscape: coils, vaporizers, sprays, nets, electronic devices, natural remedies
3. Identify which brands surface organically in consumer conversation
4. Surface safety and health concerns around chemical repellents (DEET, transfluthrin, prallethrin), especially for children and babies
5. Explore the natural vs chemical repellent tension and what drives consumer choice
6. Identify unmet needs and product innovation opportunities
7. Understand use context segmentation: indoor nighttime vs outdoor/hiking vs travel vs baby/child protection
8. Assess how disease prevention (dengue, malaria) fear drives repellent purchase vs simple comfort/annoyance
