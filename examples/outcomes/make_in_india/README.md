## Brief

The research brief is at [`../../briefs/make_in_india_brief.json`](../../briefs/make_in_india_brief.json).

**Research objectives:**
1. Understand public sentiment toward the Make in India initiative - is it seen as succeeding or failing?
2. Map consumer purchase behaviour - are people actually buying Indian over imported? What drives the choice?
3. Assess Vocal for Local adoption - has it translated into sustained behaviour change or is it performative?
4. Identify quality perception gaps - how do consumers perceive Made in India product quality vs. imports?
5. Surface sector-specific perceptions - which sectors are seen as MII successes vs. failures?
6. Understand how US-India tariff tensions and trade war have shaped the Make in India conversation in 2025
7. Explore the nationalism vs. pragmatism tension - where does patriotic buying end and practical choice begin?
8. Assess how Indian startups, D2C brands, and homegrown companies are perceived in this conversation
