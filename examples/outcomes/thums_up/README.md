## Brief

The research brief is at [`../../briefs/thums_up_brief.json`](../../briefs/thums_up_brief.json).

**Research objectives:**
1. Understand current brand perception among Indian consumers
2. Identify key product associations and usage occasions
3. Find competitive positioning vs Coca-Cola and Pepsi
4. Surface any product quality or safety concerns
5. Discover untapped marketing opportunities
