## Brief

The research brief is at [`../../briefs/weight_loss_brief.json`](../../briefs/weight_loss_brief.json).

**Research objectives:**
1. Understand the full weight loss conversation landscape in India - drugs, diets, surgery, Ayurveda, fitness
2. Map consumer perceptions of GLP-1 drugs (Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, Saxenda) in India
3. Identify safety concerns and side effects consumers are reporting or worried about
4. Understand cost and accessibility barriers for weight loss treatments in India
5. Explore how traditional/Ayurvedic remedies are discussed vs modern pharmaceuticals
6. Identify the role of influencers and medical professionals in shaping opinions
7. Understand how diet plans and lifestyle approaches are discussed alongside drug treatments
8. Surface demographic and regional differences in weight loss attitudes across India
